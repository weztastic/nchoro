Nchoro Amarachi style

A relay-sowing mancala from Nigeria. Pick a pit on your side and sow its seeds anticlockwise. If your last seed lands in a filled pit, pick those up and keep going. Any pit that reaches exactly 4 is taken at once by its owner, except that a last seed making 4 is yours to take, on either side. The game ends when 4 or fewer seeds are left.

Full rules
  1. The board has 2 rows of 6 pits. Each player owns the row nearest them. Every pit starts with 4 seeds (48 in total).
  2. On your turn, choose any non-empty pit on your side. Take all its seeds and sow them anticlockwise, one per pit.
  3. On a full lap of 12 or more seeds, the pit you started from gets a seed too.
  4. Whenever a seed you sow brings a pit to exactly 4, the owner of that pit takes the 4 seeds straight away, even if it's your opponent. Then you keep sowing.
  5. The last seed is the exception. If it makes a pit exactly 4, on either side, you take them and your turn ends.
  6. If the last seed lands in an empty pit, your turn ends. Otherwise pick up everything in that pit and keep sowing (a relay).
  7. If the player whose turn it is has no seeds on their side, they pass and the other player plays again.
  8. When 4 or fewer seeds are left after a turn, the game ends. Each player takes the seeds on their own side. Most seeds wins; 24–24 is a draw.
  9. Computer safety limits: a relay that runs for 200 laps is stopped there, and a game ends after 300 moves.
